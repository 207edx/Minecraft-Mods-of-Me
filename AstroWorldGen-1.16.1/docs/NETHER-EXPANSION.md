# Nether Expansion

## Direction

The Nether should feel larger and more varied while retaining the identity of its existing 1.16.1 biomes. New regions must blend into Crimson Forest, Warped Forest, Soul Sand Valley, Basalt Deltas and Nether Wastes rather than simply replacing them.

## New biome concepts

### 1. Ashen Wastes
- Broad dark ash plains, ash drifts and weathered basalt.
- Sparse dead trees, ash shrubs and exposed blackstone shelves.
- Buried ruins, ash-covered camps and rare mineral deposits.
- Open sightlines broken by occasional ash pillars and ravines.

### 2. Emberwood Forest
- Dense dark trees with ember-lit bark and glowing foliage.
- Warm red/orange vegetation, ember roots and ash-covered floor.
- Fire-resistant passive or neutral creatures can be considered.
- Rare Emberwood groves and hollow-tree caches.

### 3. Obsidian Caldera
- Huge volcanic basin surrounded by steep black cliffs.
- Obsidian shelves, basalt spires and cooled lava terraces.
- Lava lakes and occasional lavafalls.
- Rare volcanic structures, dangerous paths and high-value resources.

### 4. Soulglass Dunes
- Pale/blue-gray dunes mixed with soul soil and glass-like mineral crusts.
- Wind-carved ridges, fossil formations and scattered dead trees.
- Soul-lit hollows, ruined watchposts and rare structures.
- Distinct palette from the existing Soul Sand Valley.

### 5. Crimson Mire
- Damp red fungal lowlands with shallow lava-free pools and root networks.
- Crimson fungi, hanging roots and dense vegetation.
- Small islands and muddy mineral ground.
- Unique ambient and creature ecosystem.

## Nether cave systems

- Large basalt caverns under volcanic regions.
- Obsidian chambers around cooled magma pockets.
- Ember caves with glowing mineral veins.
- Deep lava channels that connect broad lava basins.
- Rare sealed chambers with ruins or caches.
- Cave type should reflect the surface biome where appropriate.

## Nether terrain improvements

- More dramatic height variation, shelves, cliffs and natural arches.
- Larger volcanic cones and caldera structures.
- More coherent lava seas, rivers and falls.
- Basalt spires and blackstone terraces with varied scale.
- Better cave entrances and tunnels between Nether regions.
- Carefully placed bridges, broken roads and natural stepping islands across lava.
- Do not make every route depend on a generated bridge or rare structure.

## Nether structure concepts

- Ashen Watchpost: small ruined lookout buried in ash.
- Emberwood Shrine: a fungal/wooden structure with rare resources.
- Caldera Observatory: rare structure overlooking a volcanic basin.
- Soulglass Archive: hidden room complex with lore books and utility loot.
- Broken Nether Roads: scattered routes, bridges and ruined waypoints.
- Volcanic Forge: rare structure concept for processing Nether materials.

## Nether ecology

Each new biome must define:
- Passive/neutral/hostile spawn groups and caps.
- Vegetation and ambient particle behavior.
- Resource distribution and recipe purpose.
- Structure frequency and loot tables.
- Fire/lava interaction rules.
- Safe spawn/portal arrival considerations.

## Compatibility and height

The 500-block height target is currently scoped to the Overworld. Do not silently expand Nether height as part of the same patch. Audit Nether dimension bounds separately if a later milestone requests it.

## First Nether prototype

Start with one Ashen Wastes test biome and a small ash-rock feature. Verify biome registration, generation, spawning, portal travel, dedicated server loading and save/reload before building the remaining regions.
